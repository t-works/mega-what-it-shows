Lessons

  • A subject line is not a cause. Merging on one put a second bug inside a known ticket, and the ticket still says one issue.
  • A guessed field becomes a fact the moment a page prints it. The tag column has been wrong on 38 reports.
  • Count reports once: by id, not by row.
